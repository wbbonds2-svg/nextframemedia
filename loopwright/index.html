FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We do the building, connecting, and maintaining. You tell us how the work gets done today, approve the plan, and use the results.

Is this software, or a service?

A service. We aren't asking you to learn another app. We design and run automations on the tools you already have, and you get a simple dashboard and a monthly report.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Anything customer-facing or financial can require one-click human approval before it goes out. Every run is logged and monitored, and we tune the workflows as we learn your edge cases.

What about my data?

Automations run on your own accounts with the minimum permissions they need. We don't sell your data or use it to train models, and you can revoke access at any time.

How much will it cost?

Plans will be a flat monthly fee based on how many automations you run, with no per-task surprises. Waitlist members get founding pricing (30% off for life) and their first automation free. We'll share exact numbers with the waitlist before launch.

When do you launch?

We're onboarding the founding cohort in small batches, in waitlist order. Join now and we'll email you when your spot opens.
